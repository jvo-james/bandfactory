Transactions

This is your payment statement. Each paid order shows the product subtotal, the payment charges, and the full amount the customer paid.

How the processing fee works

The processing fee is 2.95% of the product subtotal. Paystack takes 1.95%, and the remaining 1% covers the MTN Mobile Money charge. The subtotal is your product sales before these payment charges.

International Payments

Keep an eye on paid orders from customers outside Ghana. See where they ordered from, what Band Factory charged in GHS, and whether the payment was securely confirmed.

What to know about international card payments

Band Factory currently charges international customers in Ghana cedis (GHS). Their bank may show the payment in their own currency after conversion. That exchange rate is decided by their bank or card provider, so it is not shown here.

For the final amount Paystack settles to you and any international card charges, use your Paystack dashboard as the final reference.

Abandoned Carts

These are customers who entered contact details at checkout but have not completed payment. Use the reminder buttons to follow up without retyping their cart.

What this page means

Needs follow-up means the shopper entered a phone number but did not finish payment for at least 30 minutes. Recovered means they later paid. Dismiss simply removes a cart from your follow-up list. The WhatsApp button prepares a message for you, but you still choose whether to send it.

Start with “Needs follow-up”. Contact people politely once, especially when the cart is recent. You do not need to change stock here because stock is only reduced after a successful payment.
