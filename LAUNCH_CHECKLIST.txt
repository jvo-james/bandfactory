BAND FACTORY LAUNCH CHECK

Before switching the site to official sales:
1. Replace the Paystack test public key in config.js with the live public key.
2. Add PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY and the Firebase Admin environment variables in Netlify.
3. Replace the pickup address placeholder in config.js.
4. Replace the Instagram and TikTok placeholder links in config.js.
5. Publish the updated FIRESTORE_RULES.txt rules in Firebase Console.
6. Connect bandfactory.store in Netlify and make it the primary domain.
7. Submit https://bandfactory.store/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
8. Test one small live order and confirm: payment, confirmation, email, transaction row, order row, and stock reduction.
