Please read before you order

Distributor terms and conditions.

These are the general terms for distribution enquiries and orders. We will confirm the details that apply to your order with you on WhatsApp.

1

Enquiries and approval

All interested applicants should contact Band Factory directly. Sending an enquiry does not automatically make you an approved distributor. We will confirm in writing if your application is accepted.

2

Products, prices and order quantities

Product availability, distributor prices and any minimum order quantities will be discussed with you before you place an order. Your written quote will show the products, quantities and prices agreed for that order.

3

Payment and order confirmation

Please check your order summary before making payment. Payment instructions and any payment deadline will be sent through the official Band Factory WhatsApp contact. Only use payment details confirmed by our team through that contact. We will confirm your order once payment has been received and verified, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

4

Availability, delivery and collection

Orders depend on stock and production availability. Delivery fees, collection details and estimated fulfilment dates will be agreed with you before payment. Pre-order items may have a different fulfilment date.

5

Reselling and use of the Band Factory name

You may resell genuine Band Factory products as part of your own business. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, you are not an employee, agent or exclusive representative of Band Factory. Please do not alter our logo or use our name in a way that suggests an arrangement we have not approved.

6

Order problems, returns and cancellations

Contact us promptly on WhatsApp if an order is missing items or arrives damaged or incorrect. Include your order details and clear photos where helpful. Return, exchange or cancellation requests will be reviewed against the order confirmation, Band Factory policies and applicable law. Please raise any questions before accepting or paying for an order.

7

Changes and special arrangements

Any special arrangement, such as credit terms or an exclusive area, must be approved by Band Factory in writing. The written quote and order confirmation will set out the details that apply to each order.

This page is a guide to the programme, not a price list or a guarantee of distributor approval. Please confirm the details of your order with Band Factory before making payment.
