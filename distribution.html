Please read before enquiring

Distributor terms
& conditions.

These programme terms explain how distributor enquiries and orders are handled. Any product-specific or commercial details will be confirmed with you in writing on WhatsApp before payment.

01

Enquiries, eligibility and approval

All interested applicants must contact Band Factory directly. Sending an enquiry does not automatically make an applicant an approved distributor. A distributor relationship begins only when Band Factory confirms acceptance in writing.

02

Products, pricing and minimum quantities

Product range, availability, distributor pricing, minimum order quantities and any volume arrangements are confirmed individually by the Band Factory team. Do not rely on an estimate until you have received and accepted a written quote for your order.

03

Order confirmation and payment

Before paying, review the written order summary, including products, quantities, prices and fulfilment arrangements. Payment instructions and any applicable payment deadlines will be provided through the official Band Factory WhatsApp contact. Only use payment details confirmed through that official contact. An order is treated as confirmed only after the agreed requirements have been met and payment has been verified, unless different written terms have been approved by Band Factory.

04

Availability, delivery and collection

Orders are subject to stock and production availability. Delivery fees, collection arrangements and estimated fulfilment timelines depend on the order and destination and will be agreed with you before payment. Pre-order items may have a separate fulfilment date.

05

Resale and brand representation

Distributors may resell genuine Band Factory products but operate their own independent businesses. Unless Band Factory expressly agrees otherwise in writing, distributors are not employees, agents or exclusive representatives of Band Factory and must not imply that they are. Band Factory names, logos and other brand assets remain the property of their respective owner and may not be altered or used in a misleading way.

06

Order issues, returns and cancellations

Contact Band Factory promptly on WhatsApp about missing, incorrect or damaged items, and include your order details and clear photos where relevant. Any cancellation, exchange or return request will be reviewed against the confirmed order details and applicable Band Factory policies and law. Please raise questions about an order before accepting or paying for it.

07

Written agreements and changes

Any special arrangement, including credit terms, territory exclusivity or a variation to these programme terms, is valid only when expressly approved by Band Factory in writing. The written quote and order confirmation set out the commercial details that apply to each accepted order.

This page is a programme overview, not a price list or a guarantee of distributor appointment. Confirm the current terms for your proposed order directly with Band Factory before making payment.
